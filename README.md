# Whitecoat Map

**The hidden curriculum of getting into medical school in Texas, written down.**

A free, Texas-specific resource for the student with no advisor, no premed friends, and no map — first-generation, non-traditional, rural, inner-city, or community-college-routed. Verified admissions data drawn out visually, plus stage-by-stage guides that say what the data means and what to do next.

## Principles

- **Free, no accounts, no upsell.** Nothing is collected and nothing is sold.
- **Every fact is sourced.** No statistic, deadline, or requirement publishes without a source URL and a verified-on date.
- **Position, never verdict.** The site shows a student where they stand in the data. It never shows a probability, a score, or a judgment about whether they should continue.
- **No AI-generated content reaches users.** AI assists internally; humans verify everything against primary sources before it publishes.
