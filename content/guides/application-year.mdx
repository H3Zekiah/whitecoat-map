The application year is long, mostly quiet, and hard on the nerves. Knowing
the shape of it helps.

## The order of events

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| May 1 | Application opens |
| May 15 | Submission opens — submit early |
| August 3 | Early Decision deadline |
| October 1 | Regular deadline, 11:59 p.m. Central |
| October 15 | Letters due; schools begin making offers |
| Fall–winter | Interviews |
| January 29 | Your rankings due, 5 p.m. Central |
| February 12 | Match results |

<Source id="tmdsas-deadlines" />

## What actually matters now

**Submitting early.** Schools review as applications arrive. Complete in June
is meaningfully different from complete in late September.

**Being complete.** An application missing a letter is not "submitted" in any
useful sense. Track every piece.

**Interview preparation that is not memorization.** You will be asked why
medicine, why this school, and to talk about something on your application.
Practice out loud, ideally with a person who will tell you the truth.

## What to do this month, whichever month it is

- **May–June:** submit. Then check that every letter has actually arrived.
- **July–September:** complete anything outstanding. Keep working or
  volunteering — the year is not a holding pattern, and a gap in activity is
  visible.
- **October–January:** interviews. Say yes to every one you can afford, and
  ask about travel assistance or virtual options if cost is a barrier.
- **January:** rank honestly. See below.
- **February:** results.

## The match, and what "rank honestly" means

Texas uses a match rather than ordinary rolling acceptances. You rank schools
in your true order of preference; schools rank applicants; an algorithm pairs
you. <Source id="tmdsas-deadlines" />

Rank the schools in the order you actually want to attend. Do not rank a
school lower because you think you cannot get in, and do not rank one higher
as a strategy. The system is built so honest ranking is also optimal ranking.

## Common mistakes

- **Waiting for a perfect application in September** rather than submitting a
  strong one in June.
- **Declining interviews over cost without asking.** Many schools have
  assistance or virtual options and do not advertise them.
- **Going silent after submitting.** Update schools on genuinely new
  achievements if they accept updates.

<Callout variant="note" title="On the waiting">
  Most of this year is silence. Silence is not rejection, and other people's
  timelines on the internet are not yours — schools extend offers on very
  different schedules. If the waiting is affecting your health, that is a
  real thing to take seriously, not a weakness.
</Callout>

## Texas specifics

- The Texas application fee is a flat **$235** regardless of how many Texas
  schools you select, and **TMDSAS does not grant fee waivers**.
  <Source id="tmdsas-about" /> Budget for it.
- Because the fee is flat, applying broadly within Texas costs nothing extra.
- **Early Decision is binding.** If accepted, you commit and withdraw
  elsewhere. You learn on October 1. <Source id="tmdsas-deadlines" />
