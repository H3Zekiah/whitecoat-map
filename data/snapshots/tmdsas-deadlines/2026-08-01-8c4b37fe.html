Entry Year 2027

Dental Application

Accordion Open/Close Toggle

May 1

TMDSAS application becomes available at 8 a.m. CST

May 15

TMDSAS application submission opens at 8 a.m. CST

October 1

Submission deadline for application to the dental and medical programs

All sections of the application must be complete and the application must be submitted by 11:59 p.m. CST. TMDSAS does not grant deadline extensions under any circumstances.

Payment must be entered and submitted in order to submit your application by the deadline.

October 15

Medical and Dental Applicants: Letters of evaluation should be uploaded or post-marked by this date.

TMDSAS will still update this section and accept letters after this date, but it is up to the discretion of the schools if they choose to accept them. *Some schools will not extend an interview offer until all letters of evaluation and test scores have been received.*

December 15

Dental schools begin extending offers of acceptance.

April 15

Dental schools can no longer make offers to applicants holding another seat

Medical Application

Accordion Open/Close Toggle

May 1

TMDSAS application becomes available at 8 a.m. CST

May 15

TMDSAS application submission opens at 8 a.m. CST

August 3 

Submission deadline for application to the following programs: 

  • TTHSC SOM AAMC Early Decision Program 
  • UNTHSC TCOM Early Decision Program
  • UTRGV SOM Early Decision Program   
  • TAMU SOM Partnership for Primary Care Program (PPC) 
  • TAMU SOM Premed Fellows Early Admission Program (PMF) 
  • TAMU SOM Engineering to Medicine Early Admission Program (E2M)

Learn more about Special Programs. 

August 17

All supporting documents (transcripts, evaluation letters, test scores) for Early Decision Program applicants must be received at TMDSAS. 

*Failure to submit documents by these dates may result in disqualification from Early Decision Program consideration.*

October 1

Submission deadline for application to the dental and medical programs

All sections of the application must be complete and the application must be submitted by 11:59 p.m. CST. TMDSAS does not grant deadline extensions under any circumstances.

Payment must be entered and submitted in order to submit your application by the deadline.

October 1

Early Decision Program decisions announced 

October 15

Medical schools begin extending offers of acceptance

October 15

Medical and Dental Applicants: Letters of evaluation should be uploaded or post-marked by this date.

TMDSAS will still update this section and accept letters after this date, but it is up to the discretion of the schools if they choose to accept them. *Some schools will not extend an interview offer until all letters of evaluation and test scores have been received.*

January 29

Submission deadline for RANKING of SCHOOL PREFERENCE for the TMDSAS admissions match; must be entered online by 5 p.m. CST

February 12

Match results announced and rolling admissions period begins

April 30

Medical applicants with multiple offers must decide which program to attend and withdraw from other schools

May 15

Medical schools can no longer make offers to Texas applicants holding another seat

Veterinary Application

Accordion Open/Close Toggle

May 1

TMDSAS application becomes available at 8 a.m. CST

May 15

TMDSAS application submission opens at 8 a.m. CST

August 31*

Submission deadline for application to veterinary schools by 11:59 pm CST. 

Veterinary electronic evaluation forms must be submitted by this date.

TMDSAS will still update this section and accept evaluations after this date, but it is up to the discretion of the schools if they choose to accept them.

* The Veterinary application deadline is at the end of August; if on a weekend, the deadline will be the Friday before.

Dec. - Jan.

Veterinary school interviews begin

Check the individual schools' websites for more information.

Jan. - Feb.

Veterinary schools begin extending offers of acceptance

Check the individual schools' websites for more information.

April 15

Deadline to accept extended veterinary school offers
