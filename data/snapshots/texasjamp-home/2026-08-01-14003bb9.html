Making the path to medical school a reality for Texans

Joint Admission Medical Program is an undergraduate pipeline program created by the Texas Legislature to close the gaps between participation and success for economically disadvantaged Texans pursuing their dreams of becoming a doctor.

JAMP is the only program of its kind to forge collaborative partnerships with 68 undergraduate colleges/universities and 📍 14Texas medical schools to improve medical school access for underserved students.

Discover new opportunities and cultivate experience

From JAMP Scholar to Medical Student

Gratzelly Marquez


“I will forever be grateful to JAMP for the incredible opportunities, the life-long friends, and for helping me get into medical school. As a first-generation Mexican-American, this is something that I could’ve only dreamt of for many years. I’m beyond excited to begin my journey to become a physician and dedicate my life to caring for a diverse population!”


Build support communities with others who share your passion

From JAMP Scholar to Medical Student

Sena Saygili


“My future would no longer be determined by socioeconomic obstacles thanks to JAMP. I would not be who I am and where I am today without the support I received from this program. Countless doors are now open for me, and I now hope to pass forward JAMP’s mission and do the same for my future patients.”

Access to mentorship from medical professionals

From JAMP Scholar to Medical Student

Roberto A. Figueroa Soto


“JAMP gives me the opportunity to shadow physicians, do volunteer services, connect with medical faculty, and receive mentorship. I also had the opportunity to attend internships with The University of Texas Rio Grande Valley School of Medicine and McGovern Medical School. I am so blessed to have many inspiring people and mentors at St. Mary’s University and in JAMP.”


Support to help you navigate your journey with confidence

From JAMP Scholar to Medical Student

Lucero Grimaldo


“As a first generation Mexican-American student, my path to medicine has not been easy. Being the first in my family to attend college and purse medical school came with a lot of challenges. I have spent a good amount of life scared, confused, and lost. Throughout it all, I have remained motivated and passionate in hopes of one day making medicine a better place for everyone. JAMP has been the helping hand I needed. They help me make my lifelong dream of becoming a physician a reality.”
